# Forward-Born Truth

## Center

**History informs. Active work gets enough truth to move. New work is born correctly.**

Stage Presence OS does not require legacy Goodshuffle history to be reconstructed into perfect modern records. Historical evidence remains useful, active inherited work is strengthened only when a current decision needs it, and every new Engagement should enter the canonical Stage Presence model without manufacturing certainty.

## Capture rule

A source is evidence before it is interpretation.

A freeform note, photograph, voice recording, email, or pasted message must remain preserved as the source that was actually received. The system may later derive candidate structure from that source, but it must not silently turn the entire source into a customer request, verified requirement, confirmed date/time, or technical solution.

For the current private Quick Capture surface:

- a freeform note is stored as source evidence;
- a photographed lead sheet remains private source evidence;
- `customer_request` is populated only when the user explicitly supplies the customer's request;
- a known date may be stored as date-only truth;
- unknown time remains unknown rather than becoming midnight/noon;
- venue and next movement remain optional;
- no customer Party is created automatically until matching/deduplication exists at the capture seam;
- no AI interpretation currently promotes candidates into canonical truth.

## Why this matters

Goodshuffle history taught us what happens when operational data is useful but shaped by a system that does not match the future model. Stage Presence OS should not create a new generation of reconstruction burden.

The desired direction is:

**capture once → preserve source → interpret candidates → match existing reality → promote supported truth → ask only material questions → move the Engagement**

## Legacy vs active vs new

### Historical intelligence

Preserve evidence useful for relationship history, venues, configurations, pricing references, recurrence, and learning. Do not ask people to complete old records merely because new fields exist.

### Active inherited work

Strengthen only truth that affects current commercial, capacity, payment, or delivery decisions. Prefer source recovery over re-entry.

### New work

Create according to the current Stage Presence model from inception. Preserve the source before interpretation and let uncertainty remain explicit.

## Product test

A new Engagement should be able to begin from one sentence or one photograph without requiring Greg to administer a CRM and without the system claiming more knowledge than the source supports.

The backend may remain incomplete after capture. That is acceptable. The failure condition is not missing fields; it is manufactured certainty or unnecessary reconstruction.
