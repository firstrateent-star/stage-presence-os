# Capability Economics

Stage Presence capability is larger than owned inventory.

It includes owned Resources, subcontracted capacity, partners and venue-supported infrastructure. Operational identity, sourcing, availability, actual use and economics remain distinct truths.

The Capability surface derives from existing canonical sources:

- `resources` — capability identity, sourcing and quantity evidence;
- `resource_commitment_current_v` — active holds/reservations/allocations;
- `resource_usage` — actual observed use;
- `engagement_revenue_sources_v` — committed commercial-line value linked to Resources;
- `engagement_cost_breakdown_v` — actual direct costs explicitly linked to Resources;
- `resource_economy_current_v` — ownership/value/financing/maintenance snapshots.

The UI intentionally does **not** infer:

- utilization percentages without enough actual-use history;
- ROI from commercial-line revenue;
- margin from Resource-linked revenue minus Resource-linked cost;
- ownership from inventory identity;
- capacity availability from represented quantity alone.

Commercial-line value is evidence that the Resource appears in committed commercial scope. It is not causal revenue attribution.

## Vlourish principle under test

**A capability system should unite operational and economic evidence without collapsing distinct meanings into a single score.**
