# Greg Experience — Intuitive Business Reality

**Status:** Active product contract
**Center:** Stage Presence business model above software vocabulary

## Purpose

The Stage Presence OS backend may be sophisticated. Greg's primary experience should feel like Stage Presence itself, not like a database, CRM, decision engine, or diagnostic console.

The governing rule is:

> **Complexity belongs underneath. Greg sees business state, consequential exceptions, and next movement—not ontology.**

The system should become more technically rigorous underneath while becoming simpler to use above.

## What Goodshuffle is for

Goodshuffle is historical/current operating evidence already in motion. It may tell Stage Presence OS:

- customers and contacts
- existing projects
- dates and venues
- quoted/signed commercial evidence
- configured equipment/services
- historical pricing/configuration
- recurring customer/job patterns

Goodshuffle does **not** define:

- the canonical Stage Presence workflow
- the new status model
- capacity/reservation semantics
- the Greg UI
- customer/relationship ontology
- future quoting, operations, or learning architecture

Legacy evidence is translated into the Stage Presence model; the Stage Presence model is not translated into Goodshuffle.

## Greg's primary questions

The Today surface should answer, in roughly this order:

1. **Does anything actually need Greg?**
2. **What committed work is coming next?**
3. **What sales opportunities are active?**
4. **What is the team already handling?**
5. **Is scarce capacity creating a real concern?**
6. **Which relationships are becoming strategically important?**
7. **What financial truth is trustworthy enough to show?**

If a backend concept cannot improve one of these questions, it should normally stay out of Greg's primary view.

## Surface language

Prefer business language:

- Needs You
- Next Up
- Sales
- Team Handling
- Capacity
- Relationships
- Known committed value
- History to Resolve

Avoid exposing internal vocabulary by default:

- decision evidence state
- requirement_window_state
- provenance mechanics
- source-recovery strategy
- resolution-disposition taxonomy
- epistemic internals

These concepts remain available to system/admin tooling and Engagement-level evidence when useful.

## Emotional target

> **Everything is handled unless the system tells me otherwise.**

A quiet screen is success. The OS should not generate anxiety merely because incomplete evidence exists somewhere in the backend.

## Active vs historical Goodshuffle reality

Imported data should be treated in three broad buckets:

### Active Reality

Current opportunities and upcoming/active committed work. Fill only the blanks that can materially improve present decisions or continuity.

### Historical Intelligence

Past work retained for customer history, recurrence, venue memory, solution patterns, pricing evidence, capacity learning, and future archetypes. Do not make Greg complete old records merely to satisfy the new schema.

### Legacy Noise

Old workflow artifacts that do not improve the Stage Presence model. Preserve source provenance when appropriate, but do not reproduce the old system's accidental structure.

## Backend relationship

The Greg experience may be simple because the backend preserves:

- persistent Engagement reality
- Parties/relationships
- facts, uncertainty, and provenance
- configured solutions
- capacity requirement windows and sourcing
- commercial/commitment/operational state
- financial facts
- event history
- learning closeouts
- Decision Resolver / Exception Engine logic

Simple UI must never mean simplified truth.

## Financial restraint

Do not show guessed pipeline dollars or inferred payment truth.

Financial summaries should be visibly limited to evidence the OS can support. For example, known non-conflicting committed contract totals may be displayed as **Known committed value** while open proposal value remains absent until source evidence is recovered.

## Product test

A successful Greg Today screen should pass this test:

> **Could Greg open Stage Presence OS for twenty seconds and know what the company needs from him and what is moving without understanding the system architecture?**

If no, simplify the surface before adding more business concepts to it.
