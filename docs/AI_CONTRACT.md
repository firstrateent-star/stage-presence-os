# Engagement Interpreter Contract

## Purpose
Translate natural Stage Presence input into reviewable candidate structure. The AI is an interpreter/advisor, not business authority.

## Inputs
- typed natural language
- future photo/image contents
- future voice transcript
- source artifact references
- existing customer/resource context when available

## Candidate output
- proposed engagement name/type
- proposed event timing/location
- candidate parties and roles
- literal customer request
- inferred desired outcome
- candidate facts with category/kind/certainty/confidence/source
- candidate resource matches
- material unknowns
- suggested next movement
- contradictions/warnings

## Trust membrane
- Preserve verbatim customer statements when material.
- AI inference must remain distinguishable from verified facts.
- High-confidence low-consequence candidates may be quick-confirmed.
- Medium-confidence items require visible review.
- Low-confidence material should not silently become structured truth.
- Commercial, technical, safety, pricing, capacity, or contractual commitments require human/rule authority.
- No paid external model in First Breath unless separately authorized.

## Fast path / deep path
Unknowns do not automatically block progress. Routine requested-item engagements should remain fast. Complex/risky engagements can invoke deeper discovery.
