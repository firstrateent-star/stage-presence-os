# Stage Presence OS — Engagement Experience

**Status:** Canonical product contract  
**Center:** Intuitive Business Reality  
**Purpose:** Keep the Engagement interface aligned with the Stage Presence business model rather than exposing backend ontology as the primary user experience.

## Core rule

> **An Engagement should read like a piece of business. The backend may be sophisticated; the first human view should explain what is happening.**

Goodshuffle and other legacy systems are evidence sources, not interaction templates.

## Three layers

### 1. Business Story

The primary Engagement view should answer, in human language:

- Who are we working with?
- When and where is this happening?
- What does the customer need?
- Where does the commercial relationship stand?
- What solution is currently represented?
- Is capacity creating a real concern?
- What happens next?

Only trustworthy financial and capacity conclusions may be surfaced. Unknown remains legitimate.

### 2. Working Details

Evidence, structured facts, unknowns, Party links, Resource links, next-move controls, activity history, and capacity defaults remain available for users who need to work on or inspect the underlying reality.

They should not dominate the default Greg-facing experience.

### 3. Evidence / provenance substrate

Source artifacts, certainty states, history, inferred vs verified capacity windows, sourcing evidence, financial facts, and other epistemic mechanics remain the foundation that makes the Business Story trustworthy.

The surface may simplify wording. It must never simplify truth by inventing certainty.

## Business Story order

Preferred order:

1. Engagement identity
2. Customer
3. Date / venue
4. Commercial position and trustworthy value
5. Customer need / desired outcome
6. Current solution
7. Capacity state only where meaningful
8. Next movement
9. Relationship/history context as evidence grows

## Human-language translations

Prefer:

- Proposal sent
- Signed
- Confirmed
- Waiting on customer
- No known capacity conflict
- Watch before committing more
- Protect delivery

over exposing internal state vocabulary as the primary copy.

Internal states remain inspectable in Working Details.

## Goodshuffle migration rule

For imported active work:

- use real customer, date, venue, commercial and configuration evidence;
- recover important missing active-business truth where it changes current decisions;
- do not make Greg reconstruct historical customer intent solely to satisfy the new schema;
- keep historical work for intelligence without forcing retrospective completion;
- discard legacy workflow assumptions that do not serve the canonical Stage Presence model.

## Financial rule

Display money only from represented non-conflicting financial evidence appropriate to the current commercial position.

Do not infer proposal pipeline value from status alone.
Do not treat a zero or missing imported amount as economic truth unless evidence establishes it.

## Capacity rule

The Business Story may say:

- No known conflict
- Watch before committing more
- Needs capacity decision

only from the current Capacity Truth model.

Configuration is not reservation.
A WATCH is not a double booking.
Weak timing/sourcing evidence should be described as incomplete operational truth, not as a conflict.

## Product test

> **Can Greg open an Engagement and understand the customer, timing, commercial position, current solution, capacity concern, and next movement without understanding Stage Presence OS architecture?**

If not, improve the translation layer before adding more visible system mechanics.

## Direction

As the backend learns more, the Engagement story should become more useful while requiring fewer visible fields and fewer human reconstructions.

The intended relationship is:

> **deeper backend intelligence → simpler, more trustworthy business experience**
