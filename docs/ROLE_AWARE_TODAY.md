# Role-Aware Today

Stage Presence uses one shared operating backend. Role awareness changes presentation and attention, not canonical truth.

## Personal assignment outranks role lens

`daily_work_queue_v.owner_user_id` is the authoritative signal that a persisted Work item belongs to the signed-in user.

Today shows exact personally assigned urgent Work first.

If urgent Work is unassigned, the application role may determine whether it is relevant enough to surface. Showing an unassigned item does **not** assign ownership.

## Coarse application lenses

### ADMIN
Broad operating picture across commercial, delivery, capacity, relationships and economics.

### COMMERCIAL
Emphasizes:
- `FOLLOW_UP` and `PAYMENT` Work;
- `DEMAND`, `RELATIONSHIP`, and `ECONOMICS` movement signals;
- Sales and Relationships.

### OPERATIONS
Emphasizes:
- `PREP` and `DELIVERY` Work;
- `DELIVERY`, `COORDINATION`, and `KNOWLEDGE` movement signals;
- upcoming committed delivery and Capacity.

### VIEWER
Shows shared operating context but does not claim unassigned Work as the viewer's responsibility.

## Boundary

Application role is a presentation lens, not a substitute for job-specific assignment, Work ownership, authorization policy, or operational responsibility.

Different people may see different slices. The underlying Engagement, Work, relationship, economics, capacity, and movement records remain the same canonical truth.

## Vlourish principle under test

**The depth of the backend should reduce the cognitive burden placed on the human. Different roles should receive different relevant views over the same reality, not different realities.**
