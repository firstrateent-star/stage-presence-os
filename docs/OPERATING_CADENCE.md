# Stage Presence — Operating Cadence

**Status:** Working operating design; exact meeting times/owners are not yet enforced  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Purpose:** Make the OS create continuity automatically so Stage Presence does not depend on Greg remembering what to check or on meetings that merely reconstruct status.

---

## 1. Core principle

> Status should come from the system. Human time should be used to resolve exceptions and make decisions.

The cadence should be **event-driven first, calendar-driven second**.

Do not hold a meeting simply to discover what is happening. The OS should already know the current Engagement, commitment, capacity, next-move and exception state.

---

## 2. Daily command rhythm

The default daily surface is Today / Business Command.

Priority order:
1. Protect Delivery
2. Convert time-sensitive legitimate demand
3. Review Capacity Pressure
4. Verify high-leverage Capacity Truth
5. Resolve material unknown/conflicting facts
6. Protect/deepen valuable relationships
7. Review recent changes only where needed

A person should be able to open the system and understand where attention belongs without reconstructing the company from email, paper and memory.

---

## 3. Event-driven triggers

Future automation should surface work when business state changes, not only at fixed meeting times.

Examples:
- new lead/source arrives;
- quote follow-up becomes due;
- customer verbally accepts;
- contract/signature arrives;
- deposit becomes due/received;
- Capacity WATCH/HIGH signal appears;
- resource requirement window/sourcing is missing before commitment;
- customer input/content is overdue;
- job enters a readiness horizon;
- scope/change order appears;
- job completes and closeout is due;
- recurrence/renewal window approaches.

Every trigger should route to the authority capable of resolving it.

---

## 4. Pre-event readiness horizons

Exact timing should evolve by solution archetype, but the operating concept is useful now.

### Far horizon — approximately 14–21 days
Purpose:
- confirm commitment/commercial evidence;
- verify material capacity/sourcing pressure;
- identify customer/venue inputs that require lead time;
- surface unusual labor/subcontract/procurement needs.

### Readiness horizon — approximately 7 days
Purpose:
- tighten resource requirement windows;
- confirm operational owner/crew needs;
- confirm venue/access/power/content/network/logistics;
- resolve material unknowns;
- ensure change orders/commercial differences are visible.

### Execution horizon — approximately 72 hours
Purpose:
- final resource/prep/transport/crew/content check;
- no discovery of preventable major ambiguity should be happening here.

### Day-of / active
Purpose:
- execution, issue/change capture and customer outcome;
- avoid administrative distraction that could have been completed earlier.

These are defaults, not universal hard deadlines. Long-term rentals/installations require different archetype-specific horizons.

---

## 5. Post-engagement learning rhythm

### Immediate / same day where useful
Capture only operational exceptions that will be forgotten:
- incident;
- scope change;
- unusual substitution;
- venue issue;
- customer issue.

### Lightweight closeout — target within 24–48 hours
System prefills what it knows and asks only for missing learning:
- actual resources used;
- actual timing;
- major labor/sourcing differences;
- venue learning;
- financial/scope differences;
- Greg-dependence reason;
- what to change next time;
- recurrence opportunity.

The closer closeout is to delivery, the less reconstruction is required.

---

## 6. Weekly Business Pulse

A short weekly review can remain valuable, but it should be a decision forum rather than a status meeting.

Recommended questions:

### 1. What delivery must we protect?
Signed/committed work approaching in the next 2–3 weeks and material Execute Ready gaps.

### 2. What demand deserves movement?
Time-sensitive open opportunities, especially recurring/strategic relationships.

### 3. What capacity decision is emerging?
WATCH/HIGH pressure, sourcing uncertainty, quantity truth worth verifying, subcontract/buy decision.

### 4. What commercial/cash exception matters?
Signature/deposit/collection/change-order/pricing exception once those evidence feeds are active.

### 5. What did we learn that should change the system?
Recent closeout patterns, venue facts, archetype changes, avoidable Greg dependence, pricing/cost evidence.

If there are no decisions under a question, do not manufacture discussion.

---

## 7. Monthly / periodic portfolio review

Once economics improve, review at a lower cadence:
- relationship concentration;
- contribution by solution archetype;
- scarce-resource utilization/contribution;
- recurring vs one-off mix;
- full-production complexity;
- install/long-duration/service pipeline;
- subcontract/buy evidence;
- Greg-dependent load;
- recurring relationship expansion.

This is portfolio/capital allocation work, not day-to-day job management.

---

## 8. Meeting reduction test

A meeting or interruption should justify itself by answering at least one of:
- a judgment decision is required;
- multiple owners must coordinate a real dependency;
- unresolved ambiguity has material customer/economic/technical impact;
- a pattern requires system/process change.

Do not schedule meetings for information the OS can present asynchronously.

---

## 9. Greg attention rule

Greg should not be required to attend every recurring operating review if no founder-level exception exists.

The system should be capable of producing:
- “No Greg decisions required today,” or
- “Greg has 2 decisions: pricing exception on X; capacity conflict on Y.”

That is a major success criterion for reducing founder dependency.

---

## 10. North star

> Stage Presence should develop a reliable pulse without requiring a person to carry the pulse in their head.
