**Evidence date:** 2026-09-09  
**Source basis:** Goodshuffle Pro project export, 30 project pullsheets, Goodshuffle inventory export, existing Stage Presence inventory evidence, live Stage Presence OS state.  
**Purpose:** Test the current business-model hypotheses against real imported operating evidence without treating legacy-system structure as truth.

## Governing interpretation rule

> Legacy systems are evidence sources. They do not define the future operating model.

Goodshuffle tells us what project records, statuses, dates, line items, prices and customer labels existed. Stage Presence OS translates those observations into its own model of Engagements, Parties, Facts, Resources, uncertainty, commercial state, capacity pressure and relationship history.

Current structured configuration evidence:
- 20 / 30 Engagements include known VIDEO resources
- 5 include AUDIO resources
- 2 include LIGHTING resources
- 4 include STAGING resources
- 3 include POWER resources
- 18 Engagements currently appear video-led without another major technical category represented in their known structured resource configuration

Most frequently configured physical visual resources:
- 17x10 LED Trailer — 13 Engagements
- 12x7 LED Trailer — 12 Engagements

Interpretation:
This materially strengthens the current LED-first strategic thesis. It does not prove that audio/lighting/staging lack standalone value, because custom/untracked line items and incomplete exports may hide additional scope. The present evidence supports keeping large-format visual capability at the strategic center while treating adjacent capabilities according to attachment value, reliability, contribution and relationship impact.

## Hypothesis test — relationship compounding

Current client recurrence:

- that relationship carries $102,000 of the $127,804.01 currently known signed value
- this equals 79.8% of the known signed value represented by the imported sample

Relationship value is already too important to remain implicit inside individual jobs. The evidence simultaneously reveals opportunity and concentration risk. The OS should eventually distinguish contact, organization, account, venue, referrer and end-client relationships more deeply rather than treating every repeated customer label as an isolated lead source.

## Hypothesis test — scarce-capacity risk

Current configuration evidence shows repeated use of the same visual assets across both committed and open demand.

Examples of pressure signals include:
- November 14, 2026: signed UNC configuration and proposed Film Festival configuration both contain the 17x10 LED Trailer; both also reference the MB-5 Mobile Video Trailer
- November 14, 2026: proposed Holy Smokes and signed UNC configuration overlap on the 12x7 LED Trailer date range
- additional overlaps exist in logistics/service line items across simultaneous work

The physical quantity evidence for the 17x10 and 12x7 trailers is currently one each, but quantity state remains UNVERIFIED.

Interpretation:
This is not proof of an actual reservation conflict. Stage Presence can subcontract/source capability and the Goodshuffle configuration dates may not equal exact possession windows. It is strong evidence that a capacity-pressure layer should exist before additional commitments are made. The OS should show warnings without fabricating availability or reservation truth.

## Current business-model conclusions

### Strengthened
1. LED / large-format visual is the strongest observed strategic center.
2. Recurring relationships are a major value engine and concentration risk.
3. Greg's attention needs business-priority routing, not raw status queues.
4. Capacity visibility is becoming commercially important before full reservation automation.
5. Institutional memory is immediately valuable: imported project configuration can now be reused without reconstructing Goodshuffle jobs manually.

### Still unproven
1. True contribution by Engagement.
2. Profitability of audio, staging, lighting or other adjacencies independently versus as attachments.
3. Asset utilization after accounting for all historical work and true possession windows.
4. Whether long-term deployment / installation / service will outperform event-only growth economically.
5. True customer lifetime contribution beyond the limited Goodshuffle export.
6. Exact founder-hour constraint and labor-capacity bottlenecks.

## Resulting OS priorities

### Priority 1 — Business Command Layer
Home / Today should allocate attention according to:
1. Protect Delivery
2. Convert Demand
3. Capacity Pressure
4. Relationships
5. Unresolved Truth

### Priority 2 — Capture
Continue reducing manual entry so new reality enters the same Engagement system by typed note, photo and eventually voice/web/email.

### Priority 3 — Capacity truth
Move from pressure signals toward explicit capacity windows, holds, reservation evidence, sourcing alternatives and release rules only after the current pressure surface proves useful.

### Priority 4 — Relationship intelligence
Build persistent organization/contact/account relationships, recurring-event recognition and cross-Engagement history from evidence.

### Priority 5 — Economics / pricing
Before a sophisticated quote engine, acquire reliable direct-cost and contribution evidence. Quote speed matters, but optimizing fast quotes around unknown economics could accelerate poor commitments.

## Strategic caution

The imported sample is current operating evidence, not a complete company history. Strong signals should influence sequencing while remaining revisable. Evidence earns expansion; it does not grant certainty where the sample cannot support it.
