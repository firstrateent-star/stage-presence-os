# Roadmap — Evidence-Driven Petals

## Governing rule

> **Evidence earns expansion.**

The roadmap is not a feature backlog and Goodshuffle is not the product blueprint. Build only what reduces a real Stage Presence constraint, protects a real commitment, improves a real business decision, reduces reconstruction, or removes unnecessary founder dependence.

## Current transition center

> **History informs. Active inherited work gets enough truth to move. New work is born correctly.**

The system is deliberately moving through:

**Goodshuffle-seeded history → Active Continuity → Forward-Born Truth → native Stage Presence flow**

Decision-Grade Flow and Exception-Driven Flow remain backend operating principles. The human-facing product center is **Intuitive Business Reality**: complexity belongs underneath; Greg sees Stage Presence.

## 3. Active Continuity — CURRENT OPERATING RULE

Goodshuffle has already seeded 30 real Engagements. Do not rebuild them into perfect new-schema history.

For inherited active work:
- recover or strengthen only truth that changes current commercial/capacity/payment/delivery decisions;
- do not treat database blanks as tasks;
- do not force literal customer-intent reconstruction when existing proposal/configuration evidence is enough to continue;
- do not interpret absence in Stage Presence OS as proof that payment, sourcing, or operational planning did not happen elsewhere.

Current active/future slice:
- 26 Engagements
- 13 opportunities
- 13 committed jobs

Highest-leverage unresolved evidence classes:
1. 13 active proposals without typed proposal value
2. 1 active opportunity without customer identity
3. 2 without venue
4. 3 without configured solution
5. 23 with weak/inferred capacity timing and unknown job-specific sourcing
6. 13 committed records without deposit evidence in the OS
7. direct-cost evidence absent, but only acquire when economically decision-relevant

Import placeholder actions are not real continuity. `Review quote status and set follow-up` and `Review operational readiness` are now suppressed from Greg-facing business movement.

### Source-recovery boundary
The original Goodshuffle master spreadsheet contents are not currently recoverable from Supabase provenance, the active File Library, or the connected Gmail account. Do not ask someone to re-enter 13 proposal totals simply because the source is unavailable.

If the master export becomes available again, recover proposal values from source. Otherwise continue operating and let native Stage Presence evidence take over.

## 4. Forward-Born Truth — CURRENT NEW-WORK RULE

Canonical: `docs/FORWARD_BORN_TRUTH.md`.

Current private Quick Capture now enforces:
- raw note/photo is source evidence first;
- freeform text is not silently copied into structured `customer_request`;
- explicit customer request remains optional and separate;
- date-only truth is first-class at creation;
- unknown time remains unknown;
- photo-only/note-only capture remains valid;
- no automatic customer Party creation until matching/dedup exists;
- no AI interpretation currently promotes candidates into canonical truth.

Target flow:

**capture once → preserve source → interpret candidates → match existing reality → promote supported truth → ask only material questions → move the Engagement**

This is the primary path by which Goodshuffle-shaped data should become less important over time.

---

## 5. Capture Interpreter — NEXT MAJOR CAPABILITY, PERMISSION-GATED

The largest remaining friction in native capture is that one sentence/photo is preserved safely but not yet structured automatically.

Desired Interpreter responsibilities:
- detect possible existing Party/Engagement matches before creation/merge;
- extract candidate customer/company/contact/date/venue;
- preserve literal customer request separately from interpretation;
- detect service/capability/resource mentions;
- identify material unknowns;
- recommend the next decision/movement;
- attach confidence/provenance;
- ask only questions that could change the next decision.

The Interpreter must optimize for **decision utility**, not maximum field extraction.

### Activation gate
Do not enable an external/paid AI provider until there is an explicit decision on:
- provider/privacy treatment of customer source material
- cost/budget
- model/API project ownership
- retention/logging expectations
- candidate confidence thresholds
- what AI may write directly vs what requires review

Until approved, source capture remains useful and truthful without AI.

## 6. Durable Source Evidence — EARNED LESSON, INTAKE SEAM NOT YET ACTIVATED

The Goodshuffle import preserved hashes/metadata but not recoverable source-file bytes. This prevented later source recovery of proposal values.

Future principle:
> A fingerprint proves a source existed. Durable evidence preserves the private source well enough to recover truth later.

Current `source-artifacts` Storage bucket is private, 15 MB, and image-only. Do not widen it to arbitrary documents merely because the architecture may need them later.

Activate document/spreadsheet source storage when there is a concrete private import/document intake seam. At activation, support least-privilege access, actual source retention, provenance linking, and controlled parsing.

## 8. Decision / Exception Engine — BACKEND INTELLIGENCE, NOT PRIMARY UI

### Decision-Grade Flow
> What decision is next, what truth can change it, and who can resolve the exception?

### Commitment Ladder
- Quote Ready
- Commit Ready
- Reserve Ready
- Execute Ready

### Exception-Driven Flow
Routine reality should be absorbed through evidence, inheritance, source recovery, policy, or deterministic logic before reaching humans.

Resolution approaches:
- reuse evidence
- source recovery
- group resolution
- owner confirmation
- policy decision
- human judgment

Resolution Compression:
> Resolve truth once at the broadest valid scope; inherit downward; override exceptions.

The success condition is not more warnings. It is fewer unnecessary human touches while commitments remain trustworthy.
