## Baseline hypotheses to measure
- Lead/engagement capture currently requires unnecessary human reconstruction/re-entry.
- Active opportunities can lose continuity when next actions live in memory/paper/conversation.
- Greg currently carries too much routine company state.
- Inventory knowledge is incomplete and distributed.
- Existing systems do not fully preserve a single customer/opportunity reality from demand through delivery.

- Adoption by user/role
- Corrections to AI/manual interpretation
