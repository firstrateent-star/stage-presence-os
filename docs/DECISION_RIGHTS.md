# Stage Presence — Decision Rights / Exception Routing

**Status:** Working operating design; named assignments beyond current known roles require real-world confirmation before enforcement  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Purpose:** Prevent Stage Presence OS from merely identifying the right issue and then routing every decision back to Greg.

---

## 1. Core principle

> Route the exception to the authority qualified to resolve that type of risk.

The system should own continuity, memory, deterministic checks and propagation. People should own judgment only where judgment changes customer outcome, economic risk, technical feasibility, capacity or relationship value.

Do not equate “owner/founder” with default owner of every task.

---

## 2. Authority layers

### Layer 0 — System continuity
The OS should eventually handle without human judgment:
- remembering state and source evidence;
- deterministic reminders/resurfacing;
- propagating accepted facts;
- identifying missing evidence;
- calculating capacity-pressure/readiness signals;
- preparing routine drafts/checklists;
- matching prior archetypes/venue history as candidates;
- recurrence reminders;
- preserving history.

The system may surface a recommendation but does not fabricate commercial/technical truth.

### Layer 1 — Commercial / administrative continuity
Current likely roles: Nancy and/or Sean depending task.

Typical decisions/actions:
- contract/signature administration;
- deposit/payment evidence;
- invoice/collection continuity;
- standard follow-up;
- routine proposal administration;
- standard pricing inside approved guardrails once those guardrails exist;
- maintaining complete commercial evidence.

### Layer 2 — Operational / technical authority
Current role is a function rather than one permanently assigned named person. Qualified Stage Presence technical/operations people should resolve:
- actual capacity/sourcing plan;
- possession/setup/strike/return timing;
- warehouse/prep feasibility;
- technical implementation;
- crew/operator requirements;
- venue/logistics constraints;
- substitutions that remain inside approved solution/commercial boundaries;
- Execute Ready judgment for routine production.

The exact people qualified for each capability must be learned/verified rather than assumed from a historical name list.

### Layer 3 — Founder / strategic authority
Greg should be reserved for exceptions where his judgment materially changes value or risk:
- strategic/customer relationship negotiation;
- major price exception;
- unusual liability or technical risk;
- high-impact capacity conflict;
- significant capital/procurement exposure;
- complex/non-standard solution design;
- important partner/supplier exception;
- high-value commercial terms outside normal guardrails;
- capital allocation / buy-vs-source decisions.

Goal: Greg becomes strategic exception capacity, not the routine continuity mechanism.

## 3. Commitment Ladder routing

### Quote Ready
System:
- identify known/unknown/assumed inputs;
- detect prior archetype/venue/customer history;
- surface pricing/capacity exceptions.

Commercial owner:
- move routine quote forward;
- ensure customer/terms are represented correctly.

Technical authority:
- review only when technical design materially affects scope/price/risk.

Greg:
- only when strategic relationship, price, capacity or technical exception exceeds guardrails.

### Commit Ready
System:
- explain readiness evidence/gaps;
- surface capacity pressure and unresolved material facts.

Commercial authority:
- confirm scope/value/terms inside approved authority.

Operations/technical:
- confirm feasible sourcing/timing where required.

Greg:
- approve only exceptions outside established commercial/technical/capacity authority.

### Reserve Ready
Commercial administration:
- signature/acceptance evidence;
- required deposit/payment or approved exception evidence.

Operations/capacity authority:
- confirm what capacity is actually being protected, source, quantity and window.

System:
- then record/propagate reservation state and future conflict logic once the reservation module exists.

Greg:
- only for a capacity/payment/commercial exception that exceeds delegated authority.

### Execute Ready
Operations/PM:
- owns routine delivery-readiness judgment.

System:
- assembles missing dependencies, prior venue/archetype knowledge and remaining actions.

Commercial admin:
- handles commercial exceptions/change-order state that affects execution.

Greg:
- only for material technical, relationship, liability or capacity exception.

## 5. Fast / Review / Founder lanes

### Fast Lane
Routine work that fits known patterns and approved guardrails.

Desired human experience:
- system pre-structures;
- responsible commercial/ops role reviews only what is needed;
- Greg is not involved unless requested or a rule detects an exception.

### Review Lane
One or more material exceptions exist but can be resolved by delegated commercial/technical/operations authority.

### Founder / High-Risk Lane
Only exceptions that exceed delegated authority or materially affect strategy, liability, large economics, scarce capacity or key relationships.

---

## 6. Guardrails are future evidence, not invented policy

Do not yet hard-code dollar thresholds, discount limits, capacity overrides or technical authority by named person without operating evidence and owner approval.

Future guardrails can include:
- price/discount ranges;
- maximum subcontract exposure;
- capital/working-capital thresholds;
- risk class;
- capacity-pressure severity;
- relationship strategic tier;
- solution archetype confidence;
- technical qualification/certification.

Until grounded, route uncertain high-impact cases upward rather than fabricating authority.

---

## 7. System design consequence

Future tasks/alerts should carry:
- **reason** human attention is required;
- **decision type** being made;
- **evidence/gap** driving the exception;
- **authority role** capable of resolving it;
- **due timing** based on customer/event risk.

Bad alert:
> Greg needs to review SP-000123.

Better alert:
> Pricing exception — proposed price is below approved target band. Commercial authority required before Commit Ready.

Better alert:
> Capacity WATCH — 17x10 requirement overlaps signed work; sourcing/window is still inferred. Operations/capacity review required before commitment.

---

## 8. North star

> The company should not ask Greg what is happening. The system should know what is happening and ask the right qualified person only for the judgment it cannot responsibly make itself.
