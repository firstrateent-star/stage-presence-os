# Cost Rate Governance

Reusable cost rates are not historical job truth. They are future-facing assumptions that may be promoted into authority only through an explicit governed action.

## States

- `DRAFT` — evidence or hypothesis; visible for review but not automatic authority.
- `APPROVED` — authorized for future governed economic reasoning.
- `RETIRED` — no longer future authority; historical job evidence remains unchanged.

Approval is restricted to active Stage Presence `ADMIN` members and records `approved_by` + `approved_at`.

Retirement also requires `ADMIN` authority.

Historical `engagement_cost_items` remain canonical for what a job actually cost. Approving or retiring a reusable rate never rewrites earlier job actuals.

## Current live evidence

The current database contains two draft team-member pay rates at $80/hour (Scott and Ben). They remain draft until explicitly approved.

## Vlourish principle under test

**Reusable assumptions should gain authority through explicit promotion, while observed historical facts remain immutable evidence.**
